# AgriShield Privacy & Ethics Policy

## Principles & Core Commitments

AgriShield is designed specifically as a supportive, non-surveillance quality assurance tool for farmers and food-processing raw material procurement teams. The system adheres to strict ethical principles to protect farmer privacy and prevent misuse.

---

## 1. No Farmer Surveillance or Location Tracking
- ❌ **No GPS Tracking**: The system does NOT request, access, or log exact device GPS coordinates.
- 📍 **Approximate Regions Only**: Observations record only general administrative areas (State, District, Region/Block) voluntarily selected by the user.

## 2. No Farmer Identity Exploitation
- ❌ **No Personal Identifiers**: The application does not collect Aadhaar IDs, government identification, exact land titles, or personal home addresses.
- 🔒 **Role-Based Access**: Farmer observation logs are identified by anonymous/pseudonymous Observation IDs (`#1`, `#2`, etc.).

## 3. Non-Punitive Procurement Safeguards
- ❌ **No Farmer Ranking**: Disease observation records are never used to penalize, black-list, or downgrade supplier ratings.
- 🤝 **Supportive Escalation**: Early disease reporting triggers assistance from agricultural experts to help farmers treat crops before yield loss occurs.

## 4. Mandatory Screening Disclaimer
- ⚠️ **Preliminary Screening Tool**: AI disease predictions are automated initial assessments intended to triage high-risk cases for expert evaluation. They do NOT replace professional agricultural expert diagnosis.

## 5. Data Isolation Guarantee
- 🛡️ **Runtime Image Isolation**: Images uploaded by farmers during runtime (`app/static/uploads/`) remain private to their observation record and are **never automatically harvested or added** to the ML training dataset without express informed consent.

## 6. Academic and Open Source Integrity
- 🔬 **Transparent Limitations**: All experimental evaluations, precision metrics, and confidence intervals are reported truthfully.
- 🚫 **Strict No Fake Data Policy**: Prototype seed records and synthetic tests are explicitly designated as `DEMO_SEED` or `PENDING INDEPENDENT VALIDATION` to prevent academic misrepresentation.
